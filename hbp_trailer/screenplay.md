# HARRY POTTER AND THE HALF-BLOOD PRINCE
### A fan-made anime trailer · 60 seconds · 16:9 · dark seinen style

**Logline.** In a year when the war comes out of hiding, an old headmaster with a dying hand takes a boy into the memories of the young Tom Riddle. Meanwhile a frightened schoolboy works on a murder in secret, and a mysterious Prince's handwriting teaches a curse no one should know. It all ends on a tower under a green sky.

**Shape.** One continuous story told with straight cuts. There are no title cards; music and sound carry the transitions.
- **Act I, the omen (0–13 s):** the war begins in secret, then in the open.
- **Act II, the Prince (13–27 s):** the Prince's book, Draco's errand, Sectumsempra.
- **Act III, the memory (27–39 s):** Slughorn's memory, young Riddle, the word "Horcruxes". This is the turning point.
- **Act IV, the cave and the tower (39–60 s):** the price of the secret.

**Conventions.**
- Japanese dialogue, with English subtitles burned in.
- The only on-screen writing is the Prince's inscription in shot 5, which is part of the picture.

## ACT I: THE OMEN

**FADE IN:**

**1. EXT. SPINNER'S END, A MILL TOWN, NIGHT** `0:00–0:03.8`

Black. The scrape of a single match.

Rain. A narrow cobbled lane between dead brick terraces runs beside a filthy river. A disused mill chimney stands over everything like a gravestone. One upstairs window glows.

Two hooded women walk briskly up the lane, cloaks streaming water. **NARCISSA**, pale-blonde and frightened, keeps her eyes ahead. **BELLATRIX**, wild-haired, leans in beside her, urgent.

> **BELLATRIX** *(urgent, scornful)*
> シシー、待って！あの男は信用できない！
> *Cissy, wait! He can't be trusted!*

> SOUND: rain on stone, a distant dog, the match.
> MUSIC: a lone music box begins, slightly out of tune.

**CUT TO:**

**2. INT. SPINNER'S END, SNAPE'S SITTING ROOM, NIGHT** `0:03.8–0:07`

An extreme close-up of two hands clasped across the frame: Narcissa's slim white hand and a thin, sallow man's hand.

Bellatrix's wand-tip touches the knot of fingers. A ribbon of red-gold fire uncoils from it and wraps both wrists, tightening like a rope.

The camera pushes in. Behind, half a face: **SNAPE**, his lank hair curtaining his eyes. He does not blink.

> **NARCISSA** *(a whisper)*
> セブルス……誓ってくれる？
> *Severus… will you swear it?*

> SOUND: the fire ribbon hisses and tightens, then one deep boom on the cut.

**SMASH CUT TO:**

**3. EXT. LONDON, A SUSPENSION BRIDGE, DUSK** `0:07–0:09.5`

A wide shot. Commuters fill the bridge in cold grey light. Black smoke-trails corkscrew out of the storm clouds and lash around the bridge. The cables snap and whip, and the deck twists and folds slowly into the river. Tiny figures run.

> SOUND: the shriek of the smoke-trails, steel groaning, the music box cut off mid-note.

**CUT TO:**

**4. INT. HOGWARTS, THE HEADMASTER'S STUDY, NIGHT** `0:09.5–0:13`

Warm firelight, spindly silver instruments, portraits asleep in their frames. This is the first warmth of the trailer.

**DUMBLEDORE** sits behind his desk. Slowly, he lifts his right hand into the light. It is black, withered and dead to the wrist. A heavy gold ring with a cracked black stone glints on one finger.

He looks up at us over his spectacles, calm as ever.

> **DUMBLEDORE**
> ハリー、君の力が必要じゃ。
> *Harry, I need your help.*

> MUSIC: low strings enter beneath the music box, and a slow pulse begins.

## ACT II: THE PRINCE

**5. INT. HOGWARTS, THE POTIONS DUNGEON, DAY (INSERT)** `0:13–0:15`

Looking down at the first page of a battered second-hand potions textbook. The paper is yellowed and stained, and candlelight flickers across it. Steam drifts over the page.

On it, in small, cramped, slanted handwriting:

> この本は半純血のプリンスの蔵書である
> *(This book is the property of the Half-Blood Prince.)*

The camera pushes slowly in on the words. This shot is a slow push-in on the still image, not generated video, so the handwriting stays sharp. The English translation appears as a subtitle.

> SOUND: a page settling, bubbling cauldrons, a quill scratching that seems to come from nowhere.

**6. INT. POTIONS DUNGEON, CONTINUOUS** `0:15–0:17.5`

**HARRY** sits at a scarred bench with the book open in both hands, reading. The margins are crammed with corrections and notes, as if someone argued with every page. His eyes narrow. Who are you?

**7. INT. POTIONS DUNGEON, LATER** `0:17.5–0:19.5`

**SLUGHORN** holds up a tiny crystal vial of liquid gold between finger and thumb, beaming. Golden droplets leap out of it like fish and hang in the air. The whole dungeon flushes gold.

> SOUND: a high, glassy chime.

**CUT TO:**

**8. INT. HOGWARTS, THE ROOM OF HIDDEN THINGS, NIGHT** `0:19.5–0:22.5`

A cathedral of junk piled to the rafters, with dust hanging in green shafts of light. **DRACO**, thin and grey-skinned, with shadows under his eyes, presses his palm against a tall black cabinet.

A caged songbird beside him flutters and falls silent.

> SOUND: the cabinet's hinges creak. Draco's breathing is shaky.
> MUSIC: the pulse quickens.

**9. INT. HOGWARTS, AN ABANDONED BATHROOM, DAY** `0:22.5–0:25`

Ankle-deep water and a cracked basin spraying across the room. Harry and Draco face each other in a standoff, wands pointed at each other, both breathing hard. Draco's lips start to form an Unforgivable…

The camera pushes in toward Harry.

**10. INT. ABANDONED BATHROOM, CONTINUOUS** `0:25–0:27`

A close-up of **HARRY**, standing in the spray, soaked hair and glasses flashing. Without thinking, he slashes his wand and shouts the spell from the Prince's margin.

> **HARRY** *(a desperate shout)*
> セクタムセンプラ！
> *Sectumsempra!*

> SOUND: the spell-crack and a burst of shattering glass on the cut. We never see what it does to Draco.

**HARD CUT TO:**

## ACT III: THE MEMORY

**11. INT. SLUGHORN'S OFFICE, NIGHT** `0:27–0:30`

Candlelight and clutter. The smile is gone. Slughorn is sweating, and his wand-tip trembles at his temple. He draws out a long, luminous silver thread of memory and lets it fall into a vial.

> **SLUGHORN** *(barely audible, ashamed)*
> ……すまない。
> *I'm sorry.*

**12. INT. HEADMASTER'S STUDY, THE PENSIEVE, NIGHT** `0:30–0:32`

Looking straight down into a shallow stone basin of swirling silver light. Harry's face leans in at one rim and Dumbledore's at the other, both underlit.

The camera falls into the basin. The silver turns to fog…

**MATCH DISSOLVE TO:**

**13. INT. A LONDON ORPHANAGE, A BARE BEDROOM, 1938, DAY** `0:32–0:35`

…and the fog clears to a cold grey room with an iron bed and a plain wardrobe. A boy of eleven sits perfectly still on the bed: **TOM RIDDLE**.

He turns his head toward us, too slowly for a child. A faint, knowing smile. Behind him, the wardrobe begins to rattle by itself.

> **TOM RIDDLE** *(age 11)*
> 僕は……特別なんだ。
> *I'm… special.*

> SOUND: everything drops out except the rattle.

**FOG DISSOLVE TO:**

**14. INT. SLUGHORN'S OFFICE, 1943, NIGHT (SLUGHORN'S TRUE MEMORY)** `0:35–0:39`

The party is over. Empty wine glasses, a half-eaten box of crystallised pineapple, chairs pushed back. The other boys have gone, and the clock has just struck eleven.

A **YOUNGER SLUGHORN**, with thick straw-coloured hair and a gingery moustache, lounges in a winged armchair with his feet on a velvet pouffe, still smiling from the evening.

Only one guest has stayed behind: **TOM RIDDLE, age 16**, handsome and composed. On his right hand is a heavy gold ring with a black stone. It is the same ring we saw on Dumbledore's dead hand. He leans in, polite and charming, as the camera pushes in.

> **TOM RIDDLE** *(age 16, softly)*
> 先生……ホークラックスについて、教えていただけますか？
> *Sir… what can you tell me about Horcruxes?*

Slughorn's smile freezes, and white fog rolls in over the memory from the edges of the frame.

> SOUND: the last echo of the clock's chime, then silence on the word "Horcruxes". Only the ticking remains.
> MUSIC: a single low cello note.

**HARD CUT TO:**

## ACT IV: THE CAVE AND THE TOWER

**15. EXT. THE SEA CLIFFS, NIGHT** `0:39–0:41`

Black cliffs, and waves exploding white against them. On a ledge beside a crack in the cliff, two figures stand out: Dumbledore with his wand lit, and Harry behind him.

> SOUND: surf and wind.
> MUSIC: the choir enters, low.

**16. INT. THE CAVE, THE UNDERGROUND LAKE, NIGHT** `0:41–0:44`

An endless black lake, perfectly still, with a faint green glow at its heart. A tiny boat glides across it.

The camera tilts down under the surface: pale faces, dozens of them, drifting with closed eyes.

> SOUND: dripping, and a sub-bass drone you feel rather than hear.

**17. INT. THE CAVE, THE ISLAND, NIGHT** `0:43.5–0:47.5`

A stone basin glows emerald. Dumbledore is on his knees beside it, his blackened hand gripping the rim, head bowed and shaking. Harry kneels close, one hand on the old man's shoulder, the other holding out a crystal goblet of the green potion, tears on his face.

Dumbledore lifts his head and cries out, eyes squeezed shut.

> **DUMBLEDORE** *(broken)*
> もう……やめてくれ……
> *Make it… stop…*

**18. INT. THE CAVE, CONTINUOUS** `0:47.5–0:50.5`

The lake erupts. Skeletal white hands burst out of the water and drag at Harry's robes.

Then Dumbledore rises. He lifts his wand, and a colossal lasso of fire whips out around the island. The cavern blazes orange. The dead shrink back beneath the water.

He stands alone in the fire, magnificent one last time.

> SOUND: an explosion of water, then roaring flame.
> MUSIC: the full orchestra hits.

**19. EXT. HOGWARTS, THE ASTRONOMY TOWER, NIGHT** `0:50.5–0:54`

Wind. Above the tower, a vast green skull with a serpent tongue burns in the clouds and turns everything sickly green.

**SNAPE** and **DUMBLEDORE** face each other across the top of the tower. Dumbledore is wandless, grey-faced, his back to the battlements. Snape takes one step forward and raises his wand. Dumbledore looks at him without fear and lifts one hand.

> **DUMBLEDORE** *(quietly)*
> セブルス……頼む。
> *Severus… please.*

> MUSIC: the orchestra cuts out. Only wind.

**CUT TO:**

**20. EXT. ASTRONOMY TOWER, CONTINUOUS** `0:54–0:56`

A close-up of **SNAPE**. Hair whips across his face, and for one frame something like grief shows in his eyes. Then it is gone and his face hardens. He raises his wand.

> **SNAPE** *(low, cold, every syllable deliberate)*
> アバダ ケダブラ。
> *Avada Kedavra.*

A jet of green light bursts from the wand-tip and floods the frame.

> SOUND: a rushing roar like a train in a tunnel, swallowed instantly.

**SMASH CUT TO WHITE.**

**21. EXT. HOGWARTS GROUNDS, NIGHT** `0:56–1:00`

Out of the white: the grounds at night. Hagrid's hut is burning, sparks climbing into the dark. **SNAPE**, running for the gates, turns back toward us, wand in hand, his face lit orange by the fire. The camera pushes in until his face fills the frame.

> **SNAPE** *(low, cold, furious)*
> この私が……半純血のプリンスだ。
> *I am the Half-Blood Prince.*

> SOUND: the burning hut, then a dark brass sting and silence.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

## Departures from the book

Each of these is made for a 60-second runtime.
- **Bridge (shot 3):** in the book this attack is reported, not shown. I've staged it so the war has a public face.
- **The inscription (shot 5):** in the book it is written on the underside of the back cover. Here it is on the first page so it reads at a glance. The Japanese wording is my own translation, not checked against the official Japanese edition.
- **Slughorn's memory (shots 11 and 14):** in the book Harry first sees a tampered version, then wins the true memory with Felix Felicis after Aragog's burial. We show only the moment Slughorn gives it up and the true memory itself.
- **Order:** the orphanage memory comes earlier in the book. Here it sits beside the Horcrux memory, so the trailer follows Riddle from boy to murderer in one movement.
- **Shots 17 and 19:** both lines are condensed from longer book dialogue.
- **The ending:** we show Snape cast the curse and the green light fill the frame, but never Dumbledore's fall. The reveal is from the book: as Snape flees past Hagrid's burning hut, Harry tries Sectumsempra on him, and Snape blocks it and says he invented those spells himself, that he is the Half-Blood Prince. His line is condensed from that speech.
